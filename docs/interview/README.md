# Interview materials

The three numbered Markdown documents contain the technical specification, resume wording, and engineering issue retrospectives. Use real project dates and only claim work you can explain and reproduce.

Evidence reports in evidence/ are copies of local experiment summaries. Original absolute paths inside reports refer to the board output directories. Raw images, video and full logs are not included. recent-run-summary.json summarizes the user-supplied short camera run, not a benchmark or soak test.

See ../validation.md and ../inference-recovery.md for measurement conditions and recovery limits.
